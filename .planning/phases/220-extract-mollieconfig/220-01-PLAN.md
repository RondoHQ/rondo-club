## Why Plan 01 is "FinanceConfig delegates to MollieConfig" and NOT "delete from FinanceConfig"

FinanceConfig currently has callers in 20 files. Plan 02 rewires those callers. If Plan 01 deletes the public methods from FinanceConfig, every caller is broken between Plan 01 commit and Plan 02 commit — no safe intermediate snapshot is possible. Solution: Plan 01 turns FinanceConfig Mollie methods into one-liner forwarders (`return FinanceServices::mollie()->X( ...$args );`). Plan 02 rewires callers AND then deletes the forwarders. This preserves the "each plan commit is deployable and snapshot-clean" property.
